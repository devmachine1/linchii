#Merge Resolution Engineering

When working on Git merge conflict resolution, LLM-powered
merging, token optimization, or merge pipeline architecture:

- Read and follow `.claude/skills/merge-optimization/SKILL.md`.
- Prioritize minimizing total LLM cost per successfully merged PR.
- Preserve merge correctness and validate all generated resolutions.
- Avoid unnecessary LLM invocations and full-file rewrites.
